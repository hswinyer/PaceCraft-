How this system works A. Target User & Value Model Primary User Group & Need: Amateur long-distance runners who are training for a timed road race (5K to Marathon) and need to calculate, validate, and store realistic mile-by-mile pacing splits to avoid burning out early and missing their goal times. Without structured pacing math, amateur runners frequently start races too fast, deplete their glycogen stores prematurely, and "hit the wall" before the finish line. PaceCraft turns a runner's goal time and pacing strategy into a safe, coach-reviewed mile-by-mile workout schedule ready for Garmin watches. Secondary Stakeholder: Running Club Coach / Pacing Coordinator who oversees the system, audits submitted race strategies for cardiovascular safety, calibrates pacing variance curves, and approves or flags plans before race day. Value Model (Funded by Sponsor — Garmin): Garmin funds and hosts the platform as a free ecosystem utility. Garmin bears all development and hosting costs to drive wearable hardware sales and Garmin Connect engagement, while runners and coaches benefit at zero cost. B. System Requirements ID Category Requirement / Policy / Constraint Statement FR-01 Functional The system shall create and save a new race pacing plan in D1_pacing_plans when a user submits a runner name, race distance, goal finish time, and strategy type. FR-02 Functional The system shall calculate the average pace per mile and display a mile-by-mile target split table using the variance rules in D2_strategy_curves. FR-03 Functional The system shall reject plan submission and display an error message citing BR-01 when the calculated average pace is faster than 4 minutes and 30 seconds (4:30) per mile. FR-04 Functional The system shall filter the displayed list of saved pacing plans in real time by runner name or race distance. FR-05 Functional The system shall change a pacing plan's status from "Draft" to "Coach Approved" or "Flagged (High Risk)" and update strategy curves when authorized by a Running Coach. FR-06 Functional The system shall compute and display a summary dashboard showing total saved plans, total coach-approved plans, and average target pace across all stored records. NFR-01 Non-Functional (Performance) The system shall filter and render updated strategy search results within 500 milliseconds of user keystroke input. NFR-02 Non-Functional (Reliability) The system shall retain 100% of created and updated pacing plans across browser page refreshes using localStorage. NFR-03 Non-Functional (Usability) The system shall update displayed role permissions and enable or disable Coach controls in 0 page reloads (immediately) when the global Role Selector changes. BR-01 Business Rule Amateur runners shall not target an average race pace faster than 4 minutes and 30 seconds (4:30) per mile on any training or race pacing plan. BR-02 Business Rule Marathon (26.2-mile) pacing plans that use a Positive Split strategy (starting faster than average goal pace) shall be classified as "Flagged (High Risk)" for coach review. BR-03 Business Rule Amateur runners shall not approve their own pacing plans or alter coaching curves; only a Running Coach has the authority to set a plan status to "Coach Approved." CON-01 Constraint The system shall run entirely client-side inside a standard web browser using localStorage with zero backend servers, login authentication, or paid external services. CON-02 Constraint All pacing calculations and split outputs shall use imperial mile units and MM:SS pace formatting to remain compatible with standard Garmin wearable workout files. C.
